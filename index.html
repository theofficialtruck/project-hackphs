Game over: Earth is no longer livable

Global warming has reached +20 degrees C.

At this temperature, heat and humidity together would be too much for the human body to cool itself in almost every place on Earth. Earth would no longer be habitable for humans.

Researchers estimate that about 7 degrees C of warming would first push parts of the tropics past the limit, and about 11 to 12 degrees C would do so for most of today's population.

Source: Sherwood and Huber, 2010, "An adaptability limit to climate change due to heat stress", Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.
